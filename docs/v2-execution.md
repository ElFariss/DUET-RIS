## C1 and margin decision record

C1 is complete as a fixed-action validation sensitivity audit, and it failed the registered coarse-resolution stop rule for V2 method claims. It used 5,760 spatial returned actions (12 methods × 48 frames × 10 seeds), with the 81-segment all-OFF RZF precoder held fixed on both meshes, across three validation trajectories. The evidence summary reports the action-shift distribution, ON-count association, and pairwise rank reversals. These are repeated actions nested within only three independent trajectories; do not interpret 5,760 as an independent sample size. The V1 `0.015 bit/s/Hz` audit threshold is not a general mesh-error bound. Use the 161-segment evaluator as the V2 development reference, while treating its convergence as unproven. The coarse 81-segment V1 test outcomes remain the historical registered results, now explicitly numerically sensitive in light of this separate validation audit; C1 does not recalculate or replace those V1 results.

A separate 480-frame fine-mesh development-data generation on RX6600 had produced 24/480 verified frames as of 2026-11-07 00:02 WIB. The observed 4.4-minute parallel wave implied a rough 8.3-hour remaining estimate (about 08:20 WIB), not a completion guarantee. A separate node3 refinement was also active after a memory reduction; only four frames were complete at that update. Neither run was complete evidence then, and no test evaluation is authorized by this work.

Before using any ladder result to support an improvement claim, specify a separate mesh-convergence criterion for the 161-segment reference and a smallest engineering effect of interest. C1 supplies neither a general convergence bound nor a numerical V2 decision margin. Use fine-mesh scores for V2 development and withhold the improvement claim until an independent convergence design supports a margin. Any later test audit must follow a rule frozen before test actions are scored at fine resolution and must include every primary-budget returned action, across all included methods, paired seeds, frames and test trajectories. Report the maximum observed shift and any failure; no test result may alter the algorithm or margin.

## V2 decision and reporting rules

- The independent unit for uncertainty is the trajectory. Average paired seeds and frames within each trajectory; the ten seeds and 16 frames are not independent observations.
- The primary outcome is mean worst-user spectral efficiency at the selected budget, after full objective recomputation. Report p95 complete runtime, deadline misses, objective-evaluation counts and time-to-target as secondary measures. Charge feature, inference, memory and candidate-scoring work to elapsed time.
- On development, add one L-step at a time and compare it with its immediate predecessor. Use a paired trajectory bootstrap; keep development intervals explicitly exploratory. Before the final test, freeze a separate contrast family and its Bonferroni simultaneous trajectory-bootstrap intervals. If p-values are reported, use a centered paired-bootstrap zero-null test and Holm-adjust them separately; do not derive intervals from Holm-adjusted p-values.
- Compare the selected deadline-limited method with `pair`, `me_ga`, `ga_risc` and `mlp_ga` only after exact configuration and checkpoint hashes are fixed. Report `me_ga_paper` separately with its full workload and runtime. Include the geometry-informed method only if its assumptions are shown to fit this objective; otherwise record the applicability check and exclude it as a baseline.
- The claim boundary is one simulated coupled two-user setting. Do not claim novelty before a fresh, exact-combination literature review after the method is frozen. A null result is reportable.
